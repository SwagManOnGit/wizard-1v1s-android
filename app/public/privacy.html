Wizard 1v1s — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026. Publisher: Swag Games.

What the game stores

Your progress (coins, spells, gear, levels, settings) is stored on your device. It is never uploaded except as described below.

What is sent to our server

When you use rankings or duels the game sends a random player id generated on your device, the wizard name you chose, your best level, your duel rating and, for ghost duels, a recording of your inputs (dodges and spell casts with timestamps). No email address, phone number, contacts or precise location are collected. The player id cannot be linked to you by us; it exists only so the leaderboard can show your entry.

Gameplay analytics

The game sends anonymous counts of what happens in play: sessions started, levels started and cleared, spells discovered, shop screens opened, ads watched and purchases completed, each tagged with the same random player id. It is used only to find where the game is confusing or too hard. No message content, contacts, location or advertising id is included. Turn it off with "Share gameplay data" in settings and nothing further is sent.

Advertising

The Android app shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your advertising id and coarse location to serve ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice in the app's settings. See Google's advertising policy.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive a purchase token to unlock the item; we never see your payment details.

Notifications

The app can schedule one local reminder a day. It is created on your device, uses no server, and can be turned off in settings.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Use "Reset progress" in settings to erase local data. To remove your leaderboard entry and ghost recordings from the server, email us with the player id shown in the About section of settings.
