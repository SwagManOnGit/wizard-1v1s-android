## Full description (4000 max)

```
DRAW TO CAST. DODGE TO SURVIVE.

Wizard 1v1s is a fast wizard duelling game with one twist: every spell is a glyph you draw with your finger. A circle throws a Fireball. A star drops a Meteor. A heart heals. Draw it clean and big and the spell locks onto your rival. Scribble, and it fizzles.

Your enemy fights back with bolts down three lanes. Tap left or right to dodge, watch your stamina, and time your shields.

100 LEVELS, 20 BOSSES
Climb the tower through a castle courtyard, a ruined glade, a crystal cavern and the lava sanctum where the bosses wait. Every wizard has a different hat, robe and style, and every boss has a new trick: barrages, self-healing, shields.

24 SPELLS
Start with five and unlock the rest: Lightning, Chain Lightning, Frost Nova, Mirror Shell, Phase Step, Drain, Inferno, Void Rift and more. Build a loadout of six to ten and only draw what you equipped.

GEAR AND UPGRADES
Twenty pieces of gear across wands, robes, charms and boots. Eight character upgrades. Cosmetic hats. Earn coins in every fight, from daily rewards and the daily challenge.

DUEL OTHER PLAYERS
Ranked online duels with a fair fixed build, casual duels with your own gear, and ghost duels against recordings of real players. Climb the rankings.

PS2-STYLE FANTASY, 16-BIT MENUS
Low-poly wizards with vertex lighting, chunky pixel menus, a synthesised soundtrack. Small download, no account needed.

Free to play. Optional rewarded ads and purchases. No energy timers.
```
